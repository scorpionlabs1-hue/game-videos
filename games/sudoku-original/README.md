## Rendering

`src/render_short.py` builds the short entirely in code: it redraws the in-game
screen in the app's style and animates the puzzle from the store screenshot being
solved, then cuts to the Play Store screenshots in `assets/` and an end card.
The music and sound effects are generated by the script too. Requires
Python 3 with Pillow and NumPy, and `ffmpeg`.

Play Store: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.scorpionlabs.sudokuoriginal
